Joint IOU Grid Hardening Working Group Report  Joint IOU Grid Hardening Working Group Report -  To support the identification of high impedance faults not tripped by other protective  devices, SDG&E has developed a partial voltage detection platform that uses AMI 1.0  voltage readings to determine if there is an active downed wire within minutes. The tool is  currently being evaluated by the engineering group for correctness and adjustment to the  algorithms. Upon operationalization, this tool will act as a last line of defense to reduce the  amount of time a downed line is energized, which will reduce the safety risk to the public  and reduce the possibility of the downed conductor causing an ignition. If an ignition does  occur, the location will be easily identifiable, allowing first responders to extinguish it more  quickly.
