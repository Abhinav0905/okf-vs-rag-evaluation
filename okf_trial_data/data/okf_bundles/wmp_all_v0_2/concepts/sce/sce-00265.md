These revised standards will help reduce wildfire risks by ensuring sufficient cross-arm  spacing per 115kV design criteria while utilizing more resilient TSP or LWS structures to  reduce potential issues resulting from contact from object, which were the main causes of  66kV ignition events since 2019.
