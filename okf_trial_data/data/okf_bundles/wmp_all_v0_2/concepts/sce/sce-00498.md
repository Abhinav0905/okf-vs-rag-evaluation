Fire potential index  (FPI)  Landscape scale index used as a proxy for assessing real- time risk of a wildfire under current and forecasted weather  conditions.  Fire season  The time of year when wildfires are most likely for a given  geographic region due to historical weather conditions,  vegetative characteristics, and impacts of climate change. Fireline intensity  The rate of heat release per unit time per unit length of fire front.  Numerically, it is the product of the heat yield, the quantity of  fuel consumed in the fire front, and the rate of spread.
