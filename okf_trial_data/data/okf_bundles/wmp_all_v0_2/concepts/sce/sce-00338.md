HTMP inspection scope and frequency are driven by the  Tree Risk Index (TRI) model. Please refer to 9.2.3.1 for a description of the TRI model.  Based on their TRI score, grids/circuits in the highest risk category of A follow an annual  cycle for HTMP inspections, while grids/circuits in categories B, C, and D follow a three-year  inspection cycle for HTMP.
