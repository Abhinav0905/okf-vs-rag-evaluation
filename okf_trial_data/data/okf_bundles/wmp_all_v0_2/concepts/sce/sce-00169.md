• Risk Mitigation Effectiveness Uncertainty: To the extent possible, SCE bases its assessment of activities’ risk reduction effectiveness on quantitative data. However, sometimes quantitative data is either unavailable, due to the relative newness of an activity, or only available in a small size. In such situations, SCE will rely on SME judgment and supplement with quantitative data as it becomes available. SCE takes into account the certainty of an activity’s effectiveness as it determines whether or not to deploy it and, if so, the magnitude of the deployment.
