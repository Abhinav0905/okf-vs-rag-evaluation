5.2.2.3 Risk  The electrical corporation must discuss how it calculates each risk, and the resulting overall utility  risk defined in Section 5.2.1. The discussion in this section must include at least the following:  • Overall utility risk • Wildfire risk • Outage program risk • PSPS risk • PEDS outage reliability risk R1: Overall Utility Risk  Overall Utility Risk is calculated as the sum of wildfire and outage program (PSPS and PEDS  outage) risk.
