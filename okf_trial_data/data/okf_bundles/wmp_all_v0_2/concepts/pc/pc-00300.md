However, as climate and weather patterns change, extreme weather events are predicted  to become more frequent, and the potential exists for seasonal, dynamic, and/or isolated risk events  to occur that compound or deviate from this baseline risk. Therefore, having an additional  sophisticated, dynamic risk model grounded in situational awareness is pertinent to ensure electric  utilities know when, where, how, and why to take enhanced action to mitigate the risk of wildfire.  PacifiCorp’s approach to situational awareness, which includes the acquisition of data to forecast  and assess the risk of potential or active events to inform operational strategies, response to local  conditions, and decision making.
