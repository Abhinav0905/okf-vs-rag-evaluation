This information  provides insight into how a new fire may behave. Additionally, initial attack on a new fire may  be impacted by resource availability due to ongoing wildfires in the region.  • Normalized Differential Vegetation Index (NDVI): A numerical indicator used to measure and  monitor vegetation health and density. The district-level wildfire risk is made available to the company via the System Impacts Forecast  Matrix, a five-day forecast product issued daily by the Meteorology team. The final district-level  wildfire risk forecast is shown in the “F” columns of the Systems Impacts Forecast Matrix shown in  Figure PAC 10-22.
