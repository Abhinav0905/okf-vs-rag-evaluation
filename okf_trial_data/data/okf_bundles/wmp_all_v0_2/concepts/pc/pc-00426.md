Wildfire vulnerability  The susceptibility of people or a community to adverse effects of a wildfire,  including all characteristics that influence their capacity to anticipate, cope with,  resist, and recover from the adverse effects of a wildfire (e.g., AFN customers,  Social Vulnerability Index, age of structures, firefighting capacities).
