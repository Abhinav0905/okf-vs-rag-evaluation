These may include dialysis machines, breathing apparatus or other critical  medical equipment needed. PacifiCorp will make every attempt to restore these customers as  soon as possible.  • Critical Infrastructure Customers: These are customers who use electrical power to provide  an essential service to a community. These customers could include police, fire, hospitals,  airports, television, and radio stations.
