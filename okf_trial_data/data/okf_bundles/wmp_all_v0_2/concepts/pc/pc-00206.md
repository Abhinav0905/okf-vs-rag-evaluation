PacifiCorp intends to continue quarterly desktop reviews,  which typically include a deep dive into trends and risk.  Annual Training  PacifiCorp wire inspection support conducts annual field inspector training in January. Field visits are made during the QA/QC process to further evaluate and calibrate  with the inspection crews.
