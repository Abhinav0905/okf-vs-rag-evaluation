Tracking ID: GH-01  Overview of the activity: Under the line rebuild program, PacifiCorp is also considering  undergrounding. Unfortunately, the cost of underground construction often makes it  difficult to apply on a widespread basis. Therefore, the company evaluates the potential to convert  overhead lines to underground lines for rebuild projects on a project-by-project basis. Through the  design process, each individual project is assessed to determine whether sections of the rebuild  should be completed with underground construction. For example, a more remote, heavily forested  location with few customer connections could be an ideal candidate for undergrounding.  Impact of the activity on wildfire risk: Undergrounding of electric lines and/or equipment significantly  reduces the risk of wildfires by eliminating the exposure of power lines to environmental factors  such as high winds, vegetation contact, and falling debris, which are common causes of fire ignition.
