Infrared Inspections  Initiatives: AI-07, AI-08  QA/QC Tracking ID: N/A  The infrared inspections performed on the distribution and transmission lines are to identify thermal  rises in equipment that may indicate conditions needing correction. Due to the variability in loading  characteristics and ambient temperature, the results of these inspections can differ from day to day.
