PacifiCorp’s Risk Assessment  PacifiCorp’s unique service territory lends itself to potentially catastrophic wildfires resulting from  both wind-driven events and events propelled from fuel and terrain conditions independent from  wind events. As such, the Company uses the FireSight RAIL and RAVE output to distinguish assets  susceptible to wind related wildfire risk and assets more at risk from fuel and terrain driven fires. The Wind Risk Score is used to assess the severity of a wind driven wildfire event in PacifiCorp’s  service territory.
