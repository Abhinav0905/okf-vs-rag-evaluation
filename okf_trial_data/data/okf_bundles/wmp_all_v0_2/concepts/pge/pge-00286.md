5) Pilot Sensors to Augment and Evolve the Inspection Program: PG&E’s longer-term  goal is to leverage sensing technology (e.g., pole sensors) to get real time  eyes-on-risk at targeted high-risk locations. Real-time sensing devices provide  situational intelligence on the structure by signaling that there are damages that can  lead to ignition and/or outages. Distribution overhead patrols may be executed on foot, by vehicle, or by  aerial means and are intended to identify any significant hazards.
