Overview of Protocols, Policies, and Procedures  PG&E’s wildfire and PSPS protocols, policies and procedures for responding to and  recovering are governed by PG&E’s CERP and associated Wildfire and PSPS  Annexes.187  •  PG&E CERP, EMER-3001M, Version 10, dated November 29, 2024;  •  PG&E Wildfire Annex to the CERP, EMER-3105M,Version 5, dated March 29,  2024; and  •  PG&E PSPS Annex to the CERP, EMER-3106M, Version 9, dated July 31, 2024.  Operational Flow Diagrams  Wildfires scale from the “bottom up,” working their way from the company division level  Operations Emergency Centers to the Regional Emergency Centers, and then to the  Emergency Operating Center (EOC) when certain guidance thresholds are exceeded  (e.g., customers outages, etc.).  This is fundamentally different than PSPS events, which are similarly managed out of  the EOC, but based on modelled (not necessarily actual) impacts using a “top down,”  pre-determined “time/places” approach, which dictates the location(s), duration(s) and  scale of the event.
