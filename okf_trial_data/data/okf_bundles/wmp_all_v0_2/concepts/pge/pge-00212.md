A risk reduction/effectiveness of 100 percent  means no risk remains after the electrical corporation completes the activity;  −  A discussion of how the electrical corporation considers and evaluates the  hardened status of upstream circuits/segments/spans to determine the impact of  the activity on reliability risk;  −  A discussion of how the activity impacts the likelihood and consequence of  outage program events, including whether an area would still be subject to PSPS  events after the electrical corporation completes the activity;  −  A discussion of how the activity impacts overall reliability, including how trends  are being observed.
