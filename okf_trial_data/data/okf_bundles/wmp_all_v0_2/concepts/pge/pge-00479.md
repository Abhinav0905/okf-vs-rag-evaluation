The electrical corporation must provide  at least one qualitative and quantitative target for the following initiatives:  •  Environmental Monitoring Systems (Section 10.2);  •  Grid Monitoring Systems (Section 10.3);  •  Ignition Detection Systems (Section 10.4);  •  Weather Forecasting (Section 10.5); and  •  Weather Station Maintenance and Calibration (Section 10.5.5). 10.1.1 Qualitative Targets  The electrical corporation must provide qualitative targets for its 3-year plan for  implementing and improving its situational awareness and forecasting,165 including the  following:  •  Identification of which initiative(s) and activity/activities in the WMP the electrical  corporation is implementing to achieve the stated target, including Tracking IDs and  the Tracking ID(s) used in past WMPs (“Previous Tracking ID”), if applicable;  •  A completion date for when the electrical corporation will achieve the target; and  •  Reference(s) to the WMP section(s) or appendix, including page numbers, where  the details of the target(s) are documented and substantiated. 164 Pub. Util. Code §§ 8386(c)(2)-(5).  165 Annual information included in this section must align with the applicable data submission.
