These initiatives include  VM Quality Assurance (VMQA) and VM Quality Control (VMQC). VMQA programs  ensure a representative sample of PG&E overhead facilities meet regulatory  compliance by conducting field reviews, independent of VM inspection and tree work  schedules. VMQC programs ensure recently completed inspections and tree work meet  quality standards. Inspections and tree work are assessed against PG&E’s standards  and procedures and PRC sections 4292 and 4293, GO 95, Rule 35 and FAC-003-5.
