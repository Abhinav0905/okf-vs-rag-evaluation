In 2024, we sent over 1.8 million text messages and over 200,000 e-mails  to customers who experienced outages in EPSS-protected areas.  To further minimize outage impact, our Residential Storage Initiative (RSI) program  offers a permanent battery installation for customers with Access & Functional Needs  who are frequently impacted by EPSS outages. In 2024, the  RSI program installed 1,447 batteries for qualified customers and 1,890 batteries from  inception through December 2024.
