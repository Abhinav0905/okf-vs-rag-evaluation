Required Progress:  In its 2026-2028 Base WMP, PG&E must report on its progress developing statistical  estimates of potential wind events over at least the maximum asset life for its system. Section and Page Number of Any Improvements:  Section 5.3, page 83.  PG&E Response:  PG&E confirms that it has developed statistical estimates of potential wind events over  the maximum asset life for its system. As the asset age increases, the  fragility curves adjust, leading to an increased conditional Pf at any specific wind speed.  This fragility curve, in conjunction with the exceedance curves of the wind hazard, is  utilized to calculate the annual Pf within the WTRM.
