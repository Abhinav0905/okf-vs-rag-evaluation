Failure and Ignition Causes  PG&E performs a causal review on all CPUC-reportable ignitions caused from  equipment failure or vegetation contact. The process is documented in RISK-6306P-02.  The findings lead to an evaluation of extent of condition review and corrective actions to  mitigate recurrence in the system, when applicable. Lessons learned from these  failures, for example, are documented in ACI PG&E-22-08.
