PG&E’s risk management is based on a quantitative risk assessment to determine our  overall utility risk from wildfire and outage program events, consistent with the CPUC’s  RDF. Our approach includes an ongoing effort to continuously evaluate risk. This effort  builds on an iterative process that includes risk identification, evaluation of the impact of  those risks on our system and the community, addressing those risks through mitigation  and control programs, and monitoring the effectiveness of our risk mitigation and  management programs.
